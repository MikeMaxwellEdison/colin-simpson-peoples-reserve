How to make a respectable estimate

Build Dad’s golfing life, one era at a time.

Golf habits change. Add a separate era whenever the number of games or typical score changed; gaps are welcome, but the years cannot overlap. Your eras save as you type, so old scorecards can be folded in later.

  1. 01Choose the years

    Years are counted inclusively, so 2018 to 2020 is three years.

  2. 02Set the rhythm

    Enter the average number of games and whether that was per week, month or year.

  3. 03Add the score

    A golf score is the number of shots. For example, a nine-hole average of 48 contributes roughly 48 hits per game.
