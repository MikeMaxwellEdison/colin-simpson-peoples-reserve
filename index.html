03

The heart of it all

The heart of our home team

From a blind date in November 1986 and their wedding on 25 February 1989, Colin and Ally built a life in which family was never a background detail. It was the point.

Laura and Mike grew up with a dad who made things happen: sandpits, playhouses, swings, decks and gardens — even a rabbit hutch grand enough to be called a two-storey mansion. Dad gave us his time, practical help and belief in us. Then Willa arrived, and we had the joy of watching Dad become Poppa.

The Poppa years

Little School, train outings and telling the stories properly.

Poppa’s job description has grown to include garden competitions, monkey bars, big bad wolves, the three little pigs and one very important audience.

04

A bottle made to say thank you

Colin SimpsonThe People’s Reserve

The label was designed for a 1.5-litre Brockenchack 2021 Zip Line Shiraz from Eden Valley in the Barossa — a big bottle for a big family story, made especially for Dad and meant to be shared with family and friends.

It was the intended wine and is surely a very good one, but it was not available in time for this Father’s Day weekend. What is actually inside the relabelled magnum is El Goru by Ego Bodegas: a 14% Jumilla red made from Monastrell, Syrah and Petit Verdot.

Different wine. Same Colin Simpson vintage.

This is a one-off family tribute. It is not an official Brockenchack release.

A magnum before the magnum

Five years earlier, Colin celebrated his 70th holding another personalised magnum. The People’s Reserve continues a very good family tradition.

Staff number 23 · The Elite Region · Sir Colin

From delivery boy to Hall of Fame

Colin gave 52 years to the automotive-parts industry and the Repco family of companies. Laura and Mike grew up seeing the commitment, leadership and care for people behind that achievement — and we could not be prouder of everything Dad accomplished.

19662020

His service was recognised by the Repco New Zealand Hall of Fame. Yet the strongest measure of the career is how many former colleagues still call, remember the family and make room at the next table.

The people around the table

Mates made for the long haul

Repco mates, golf mates, old friends and family friends — Dad has shown us what it means to take people seriously and keep good friendships for the long haul.

Years after retirement, former colleagues were still asking after the family. At 75, he was still heading into Wellington for what he called a “pensioners’ man date” with the golf crew.

“Not a bad looking crew at golf today.”— Colin, 30 December 2023
08

A lifetime of saying yes to the next journey

From Karori to the world

Australia at 20, work across Asia and the Pacific, New Zealand road trips, North America, Alaska and Europe — these are the journeys behind so many of the stories we grew up hearing.

The destinations matter, but the recurring feature is the company: Ally beside him, friends met along the way, and Laura or Mike joining the journey.

“This lovely lady is still following me.”

Across the years and the map, Ally is the constant figure beside him — which is exactly how Colin tells the story too.
