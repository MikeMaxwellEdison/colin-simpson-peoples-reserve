Mike · For Dad

A legendary fatherAnd a pretty good bloke.

For the support, guidance and example that helped shape a son’s life and career.

From your son

Thank you for the start — and everything after it.

“

Dad, I am so grateful to have had the support and guidance of a legendary father — and, as it turns out, a pretty good bloke. You have always backed me, helped me find my feet and reminded me who I am when life has been difficult.

My first job was a chance you gave me inside the caring Elite Region team you had built. That guided beginning shaped much more than my CV: it gave me standards, confidence, people to learn from and a Repco family that helped set the direction of my future career.

I carry your example with me — look after the people, do the work properly, turn up when it matters and keep enough humour in the day. I am enormously proud of you and very lucky to be your son.

Love, Mike
