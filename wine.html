The honest pour

Different label. Very real wine.

The original People’s Reserve artwork was made for Brockenchack Zip Line Shiraz. That bottle could not be found in time, so the finished magnum contains El Goru by Ego Bodegas instead — and Dad can now meet the wine behind the family disguise.

El Goru · Ego Bodegas

A generous red from Jumilla, Spain.

El Goru is a 14% DOP Jumilla red made from Monastrell, Syrah and Petit Verdot. Ego Bodegas recommends serving it at 12–15°C.

In the glass

Garnet red with a violet edge, with ripe red-fruit aromas, gentle toasted and creamy notes, and memories of vanilla and cinnamon.

02

How it drinks

Fruity, savoury and full-bodied, with a rounded texture and soft, mature tannins — a very comfortable match for a celebratory magnum.

03

At the table

The producer suggests pork, beef, fish, aged or semi-aged cheese, stews, tenderloin and salads. Family company remains the recommended extra.

From grape to bottle

Ego Bodegas says the grapes are picked by hand at optimum ripeness. Each variety is fermented and macerated separately with its skins for 15–20 days, at a controlled temperature no higher than 28°C.
